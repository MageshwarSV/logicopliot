# Why customers buy Cargora

## The problem
Every day, operations teams read documents - invoices, packing lists, bills of lading, air
waybills, certificates - and **type the same information into their business software** by hand.
That work is slow, error-prone, hard to scale, and it ties skilled people to data entry.

In logistics a single import job can mean 5-15 documents and hundreds of values typed into a
customs / freight ERP. A wrong country code or package unit is only found when the ERP rejects
the entry - or worse, after it is filed.

## What Cargora does
Cargora **receives the documents, understands them, validates the data, lets a person check only
what needs checking, and enters it into the target system automatically** - then tracks the result.

```
Receive ─► Understand ─► Validate ─► Review ─► Execute ─► Complete
```

## Who buys
| Buyer | What they care about |
|---|---|
| Operations head | Jobs per person per day, turnaround time, fewer rejected entries |
| Finance / owner | Cost per job, headcount growth avoided, predictable per-page pricing |
| IT | No change to the existing ERP, browser-based, secure login, audit trail |
| Operators | Less typing, a clear screen that shows only what needs a decision |

Today's market: **logistics and customs brokerage** (first customer live: air and sea, import
and export). Next industries in the vision: healthcare & insurance, banking & finance, accounting,
legal & real estate, manufacturing & procurement - see [Product vision](/knowledge/p/business/product-vision).

## What the customer gets
- **Hands-off intake** - documents arrive by email (or upload); zips, Excel, Word and images included.
- **AI that reads every document** and fills the job, with product lines and computed values (e.g. customs tariff codes).
- **Checks before the ERP**, not after - mandatory values, allowed codes, country / currency codes, customs tariff validity.
- **People only where needed** - a review screen designed for the customer's own workflow, optional second review.
- **Automatic ERP entry** - the robot enters the job into the customer's existing ERP screens; Auto ERP sends clean jobs without anyone pressing Submit.
- **Visibility** - job monitoring, failure reasons, production support app with phone alerts, dashboards on accuracy, processing time and productivity.
- **Transparent billing** - per processed page, with the usage visible to the customer.

## Why Cargora wins
1. **It executes, not just extracts.** Most document-AI tools stop at "here is the data". Cargora
   finishes the transaction in the customer's system and reports the outcome.
2. **Configured, not coded.** One engine; each customer, document type, rule set and target ERP is
   configuration. Goal: **one workflow stream onboarded in about 2 hours** from the customer's ERP
   Excel and ERP access - see [Onboarding](/knowledge/p/workflows/onboarding).
3. **Validation that knows the target system.** ERP column rules, allowed values and the official
   customs tariff are checked before submission, so rejections are prevented rather than fixed.
4. **Human-in-the-loop by design.** Operators correct; every correction teaches the next extraction.
5. **No change for the customer's IT.** Works with the ERP they already have, through its own screens.

## One sentence
Cargora transforms documents into validated data and automatically executes business
transactions in any software system, eliminating manual data entry.
