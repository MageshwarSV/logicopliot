# Cargora - Product Vision

Cargora is an AI-powered **Document-to-System Automation Platform**.

Our vision is to **eliminate manual data entry across industries**. Any business that manually reads information from printed or digital documents and enters it into software systems can use Cargora to automate the entire process.

## How Cargora works
1. **Receive** - accept documents through email, upload or API.
2. **Understand** - AI reads, classifies and extracts information.
3. **Validate** - verify data, apply business rules and perform cross-document checks.
4. **Review** - human intervention only when correction or approval is required.
5. **Execute** - automatically push validated data into the target ERP, CRM or any business application.
6. **Complete** - track execution, handle failures and maintain an audit trail.

## Target industries
- Logistics & Supply Chain
- Healthcare & Insurance
- Banking & Finance
- Accounting
- Legal & Real Estate
- Manufacturing & Procurement

## Product philosophy
Build **one universal AI automation engine**, configurable for different industries, document types, business rules and target applications.

## The vision in one sentence
> Cargora transforms documents into validated data and automatically executes business transactions in any software system, eliminating manual data entry.
